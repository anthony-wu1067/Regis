What you do
  1. In claude.ai open Settings → Connectors and add Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive (sign in with your Google account when asked).
  2. Open this page from your artifacts in claude.ai, then press the status pills at the top to connect. Approve each prompt.
  3. In Google Drive, create the folders you want files sorted into. Regis lists them in the filing rule form.
  4. Open Settings below and add your VIP senders so they are never auto-cleaned and always float to the top.
  5. Turn on a cleaning rule, press Preview, check the numbers, then Apply. Turn on Autopilot only after a rule has behaved well for a few days.
  6. Keep the link private. Only you can open this page’s data, but anyone with the link could open an empty shell.
What it can’t do (and why)
  • Run while closed. Autopilot, scheduled sends and syncing only run while this page is open in a tab. Scheduled background tasks are a paid-plan feature.
  • Send without asking. “Send now” needs a second click to confirm. Scheduled sends fire only while this page is open, and only the ones you set to send automatically.
  • Delete Drive files or empty Trash. Filing moves files and can be undone. Nothing is deleted.
  • Trash mail on autopilot. A rule may move mail to Gmail’s Trash (recoverable for 30 days) but only when you press Apply yourself.
  • Read AI balances. You log usage by hand or by command.
  • Create calendar events. Read-only for now.
